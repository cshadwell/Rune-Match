How to play

Best on a tablet or desktop. You can play on a phone, but a larger screen makes it easier to see the board and compare runes.

  1. Find matching runes. Turn over two tiles. If the runes differ, the tiles turn back over.
  2. Build your cipher. Each matching pair reveals a letter. Select View cipher to see the letters you’ve uncovered.
  3. Complete the key. Match all 26 pairs to download the full cipher. Your progress saves in this browser.
